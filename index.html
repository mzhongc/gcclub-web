关于我们About Us

GC GlowUp Club

GC 是一个立足温哥华的女性社区平台，致力于连接品牌、创作者与本地社区资源，为女性创造真实、有价值、有影响力的发展机会。

我们相信，女性不仅是社区的参与者，更应该成为连接者、创造者与推动者。

Women's Growth × Community × Influence

GC is a Vancouver-based women's community platform that brings together creators, brands, and local community resources to create meaningful opportunities for women to connect, grow, and thrive.

We believe women are not only participants in communities, but also leaders, innovators, and changemakers.

使命Our Mission

打造一个有支持感与影响力的女性社区平台，通过连接女性、创作者、品牌与本地资源，以共创、交流与社区参与的方式，帮助更多女性获得成长、机会与真实影响力。

To build a supportive and impactful women's community platform by connecting women, creators, brands, and local resources through collaboration, shared experiences, and community engagement — empowering more women to grow, access opportunities, and create meaningful impact.

愿景Our Vision

建立一个充满连接与支持的本地女性生态，让更多女性在社区、文化与商业中，拥有表达、创造、领导与产生影响力的机会。

To create a connected and supportive community where women are empowered to express, create, lead, and make meaningful impact across culture, business, and everyday life.

Core Values / 核心价值观Core Values

Connection / 连接

我们相信，真实的社区关系，始于人与人之间真诚的连接。

Real communities are built through honest conversations, shared moments, and meaningful relationships.

Collaboration / 共创

我们重视共创与协作，相信成长来自经验、资源与想法的共享。

We believe collaboration creates stronger ideas, deeper connections, and more opportunities for everyone involved.

Empowerment / 赋能

我们希望通过社区与参与，帮助更多女性获得自信、机会与表达自我的力量。

We support women in becoming more confident, visible, and empowered — in business, creativity, and everyday life.

Community Impact / 社区影响力

我们鼓励更多人参与社区，并相信真实而持续的行动能够带来长期影响。

We believe community grows when people actively participate, support one another, and contribute with intention.

我们在做什么What We Do

链接品牌 × 创作者 × 社区共创Brand × Creator × Community Collaboration

搭建品牌、创作者与本地社区之间的高质量连接平台，通过活动、内容传播、社群合作与资源整合，帮助优质品牌融入社区，也让创作者通过内容与参与产生更有价值的影响力。

We create meaningful connections between brands, creators, and local communities through events, content collaborations, community partnerships, and resource integration — helping quality brands become part of the community while empowering creators to generate more valuable impact.

为创业女性赋能Empowering Women Entrepreneurs

通过主题分享、资源链接、合作机会与成长平台，支持更多女性创业者、创作者与职业女性被看见、被连接、被支持。

Through workshops, networking, resource sharing, and collaborations, we support women entrepreneurs and creators in being seen, connected, and supported.

提升女性在社区与商业中的影响力Advancing Women's Influence in Community & Business

GC 希望推动更多女性参与公共事务、社区活动与商业合作，让女性的声音、专业与创造力，在城市文化、社区建设与商业生态中发挥更重要的作用。

GC encourages more women to participate in public engagement, community initiatives, and business collaborations — so that women's voices, expertise, and creativity play a greater role in urban culture, community building, and the business ecosystem.
